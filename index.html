Keep supplies, storage checks, vial appearance, and symptom notes in one place.

Copper peptide

What you need

Supplies

Peptide vialLyophilized (freeze-dried) GHK-Cu powder.
Bacteriostatic waterSterile diluent containing benzyl alcohol for multi-dose vial use.
Alcohol prep padsFor disinfecting vial stoppers and skin when directed by a clinician.
Sharps containerFor safe disposal of used needles and syringes.
SyringesUse the exact syringe and needle sizes recommended by a pharmacist or clinician for your product.

Storage

Keep it stable

Freeze-dried vialFollow the product label for room-temperature and refrigeration limits.
Bacteriostatic waterStore unopened as labeled, away from direct sunlight and excessive heat.
After reconstitutionRefrigerate if the product instructions require it and protect from light.
28-day ruleMulti-dose bacteriostatic-water vials are commonly discarded 28 days after first entry unless the manufacturer says otherwise. Peptide stability can be different.

Visual check

Before using a reconstituted vial

Appearance only
ExpectedAppearance consistent with the product instructions, typically clear and free of visible particles.
Cloudiness or hazeDo not use until a pharmacist or clinician confirms it is expected.
Particles or chunksDo not use.
DiscolorationDo not use unless the labeled product specifically says that color is expected.
Persistent unusual foam or gelDo not use until the product is evaluated.

A normal-looking vial does not prove purity or sterility. Reconstitution and injection technique should follow product-specific instructions from a licensed clinician or pharmacist.
